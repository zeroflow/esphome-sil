## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (not in user-story form; see 01-01-PLAN.md note). This plan delivers criterion 3: restarting the simulator or the firmware, in either order, re-syncs full state without manual steps, and the firmware's API stays responsive while the simulator is gone.

<objective>
Implement the sim-bus session from protocol/PROTOCOL.md on both sides (firmware hub on sim_protocol.h with HELLO manifest, snapshot, sync, backoff and TX cap; Go server and engine with welcome, in-snapshot, manifest validation and replacement semantics) and ship `sil sim restart [--gap]` and `sil fw restart` through the control API, proven by a restart-matrix e2e test.

Purpose: BUS-02, BUS-03, BUS-01 (single active connection), CLI-06 (same vocabulary for the new operations), D-02 (restartable engine), D-05 (fw restart).
Output: updated hub, bus server, engine, supervisor, control, CLI and e2e/restart_test.go.
</objective>
