## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (not in user-story form; see 01-01-PLAN.md note). This plan makes the firmware side of the IO surface trustworthy: duty per port on the bus without duplicates, RPM as a 1 s mean (criterion 2), and loud config-time errors for a missing `channel:` or an over-long socket path (criterion 5).

<objective>
**Decision (2026-11-09, plan-check iteration 3):** the RPM sensor is a `PollingComponent` by explicit, recorded exception to the stack rule (see PROJECT.md Key Decisions and the amended rows in .claude/CLAUDE.md / research/STACK.md). Temperature, humidity and buttons stay push-only `Component`s.

Harden components/sim_bus for Phase 1: codegen validation (required and well-formed `channel:`, no duplicates, socket path at most 107 bytes and non-empty), platform split with USE_* guards so any subset of platforms compiles, per-channel output dedupe with snapshot replay on connect, and the RPM sensor as a 1 s time-weighted mean with clean disconnect semantics.

Purpose: IO-01, IO-02, IO-06, BUS-03 (codegen path validation, non-blocking hub kept). The session protocol (HELLO manifest, welcome, sync, backoff) is plan 01-10; that plan switches the hub's line parser to sim_protocol.h from 01-06.
Output: updated components/sim_bus, config fixtures, two e2e test files.
</objective>
