## Planning notes (recorded decisions and assumptions)

- **S7b criteria (RESEARCH "Spike S7 scheduling", adapted to a 25 s live window):**
  - (a) the mean of the readings is within 5 rpm of 1100. 20 readings bound the edge effect of the two half-filled end windows at about 60/N rpm; S7a checks the 1 rpm bias over thousands of readings.
  - (b) the peak-to-peak spread is at most 90 rpm (allows 10 ms of jitter, Assumption A6).
  - (c) at least 2 distinct levels appear: 1100 rpm is 36.67 pulses/s, so an ideal tach would give one level.
  - (d) step signature, toggle half (roadmap criterion 3 as amended at plan revision): peak-to-peak >= 12 rpm, and the readings below their mean and those at or above it each make up at least 25 %. A scratch emulation of the firmware window at plan revision gave a minimum spread of 15 rpm (at the half-step window phase) and a minimum minority share of 33 % over 25-reading windows for jitter 0..10 ms. The lattice half (readings on the 30 rpm grid or one phase-offset copy of it) needs a jitter-free window and is gated hermetically by S7a in 04-04.
  - The on-grid fraction is logged only (RESEARCH Pitfall 1).
- **Renumbered at plan revision iteration 2 (formerly 04-09):** 04-08 was split, and the shipped-library behaviour acceptance is now 04-09. This plan runs last (wave 8), so its "all gates on the merged phase" includes 04-09's subtests.

- **Native API observation is a recorded exception (checker, CLAUDE.md compliance):** CLAUDE.md limits aioesphomeapi to "entities are visible and commandable", and all stimulus and observation of the plant goes through `sil` CLI/JSON. S7b breaks that rule on purpose, in one place:
  - It reads the firmware's `Fan 3 Speed` sensor states through the native API. Criterion 3 is about the RPM the firmware reports and Home Assistant shows. That value exists only behind the native API, after sim_sensor's 1 s window. `sil` cannot see it: status tach_rpm is the sim-side gate, not the firmware's window.
  - `api_main.fan(3, 50)` is the allowed "commandable" use, the same as test_closed_loop_chain.
  - Everything about the plant still goes through `sil`. The fan swap uses `sil set`. The plant is observed with `sil status` sampling (ports[2].fan, rpm, tach_rpm and sim.bus_proto), once a second during the same 25 s window.
  - The exception is named in the test's docstring.
- **Fallback rule (CONTEXT carried forward: analytic quantisation is the fallback; restated at plan revision iteration 2):**
  - What it is: the analytic quantisation fallback, in the form RESEARCH §Spike S7 defines ("Analytic fallback (gate-held 1 s count)" and its decision rule). Plant.Frame of a tach fan returns the last completed sim-time gate reading (30 × the whole pulses of the last 1 s gate, held between gates, 0 before the first gate) instead of the 50 ms pulse drain. Current returns the same. Status tach_rpm is unchanged, and the protocol stays 1.
  - What failure it addresses: only criterion (b), a spread above 90 rpm, caused by when the firmware processes the 50 ms frames. A drained frame is a multiple of 600 rpm, so a frame edge that moves by J ms shifts a 1 s reading by up to 600·J/1000 rpm, and about 20 edges fall in every window. The held value changes by at most one 30 rpm step once per second, so the same J moves a reading by at most 30·J/1000 rpm (0.3 rpm at 10 ms).
  - What it does not address: the window-phase blur (readings between grid levels), which RESEARCH Summary finding 1 shows is the same for both variants, and which S7b does not gate. It also adds about 1 s of lag. A failure of (a), (c) or (d) is therefore not addressed by it.
  - Trigger: (b) fails in three consecutive `make test-contract` runs while (a), (c) and (d) pass and TestS7aFirmwareWindowKeepsSteps (04-04) passes.
  - Then: apply it. Adapt S7a to the held variant with every criterion (a)-(e) and every threshold unchanged (a held gate value mixed by the window phase still gives at most three residues mod 30, one of them 0). Re-run S7a and S7b, and add a row to PROJECT.md Key Decisions with the measured numbers.
  - Stop for a user decision, with no fallback applied, in every other case: (a), (c) or (d) fails; S7a fails; or (b) still fails after the fallback (revert the fallback edits first). Write the S7b numbers (mean, spread, levels, minority share, on-grid fraction, and the applied/reverted state) into the SUMMARY and return the plan as stopped. Do not loosen any threshold.
  - If S7b passes, internal/model/plant.go, internal/model/s7_test.go and .planning/PROJECT.md stay untouched; they are listed in files_modified only for the fallback path.
- **Test order:** pytest runs the files alphabetically, so test_tach_steps.py runs after test_contract.py and test_identity.py on the shared ct-main instance. It puts port 3 back to the default fan and switches Fan 3 off in a finally block.
