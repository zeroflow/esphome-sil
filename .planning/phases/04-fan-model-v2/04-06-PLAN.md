## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (not in user-story form; surfaced for review, no story invented per the MVP rules): see 04-01-PLAN.md. This plan delivers the web-page part of criterion 4 (airflow and power on the page) and D-14 (tach trace next to the shaft trace).

<objective>
Put the new port values on the page. The scope gains a stepped tach trace next to the smooth shaft trace in the port colour, dimmer. Each panel gains TACH RPM, AIRFLOW (m³/h) and POWER (W) readouts. The page stays a pure client: it applies the `tach` and `load` events and the samples' tach value, shows `--` for null, and derives nothing.

Purpose: MODEL-11 on the page and the visible half of MODEL-09 (D-14, D-16; 03 D-03 port colours, 03 D-13 fixed axes).
Output: ring/plot/store/view/app/index/css changes, extended uitest (plot, parity, layout, bench) and a real-bench e2e check.
</objective>
