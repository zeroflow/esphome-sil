## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (not in user-story form; surfaced for review, no story invented per the MVP rules): see 04-01-PLAN.md. This plan delivers the status and watch side of criterion 4 (airflow and power per port) and D-13's two-value readout (shaft rpm and tach rpm).

<objective>
Show what the tach reads and what the fan moves, per port, in `sil status`, `sil watch` and the history samples. `tach_rpm` is the last completed 1 s gate reading, next to the unchanged shaft `rpm`. `airflow_m3h` and `power_w` come from the fan's datasheet values by the fan laws, and are null when the fan has none. Everything is additive; Status schema stays 1.

Purpose: MODEL-11 (status and watch) and the observable half of MODEL-09 (D-13, D-16). Phase 5's dead-tach and wrong-ppr faults rely on seeing both rpm values.
Output: engine snapshot fields and events, events kinds tach and load, PortStatus fields, the fan-law derivation, a schema 2 fixture with a datasheet, and tests through the engine and the supervisor.
</objective>

## Planning notes (recorded decisions and assumptions)

- **The page store derives nothing (RESEARCH "page plumbing gap"):** the page folds only events into its status mirror. The engine therefore emits explicit `tach` and `load` events for every change, including right after a fan swap. The page (04-07) never resets or computes these fields itself.
- **Typed null:** a `tach` event for a missing reading carries V as a nil `*float64` inside the `any` field. That encodes as `"v":null`, not as an omitted key (Event.V has omitempty, which drops only a nil interface). `load` events always carry `events.Load{AirflowM3h, PowerW}` with explicit nulls.
- **Cadence:** tach readings change at most once per sim second per port (at gate ends), so tach events are bounded. Load events ride the existing RPMEventStep (1 s) sample in emitRPM and fire only on a float32 change or a change of null-ness, plus once after a swap.
- **Interface first in Task 1:** PortStatus gets all three pointer fields and the supervisor maps all three in Task 1. Task 2 makes airflow and power non-null for datasheet fans.
- **tach_rpm for ideal fans (user decision 2026-11-11, D-13 amended, A5 resolved):** Plant.Reading (04-04) is defined for every fan, so the engine needs no special case: TachRPM is nil only while Reading reports ok false (the plant's first second). Ideal-tach ports therefore emit tach events too, about once per second while the shaft moves; they are compared at float32 like rpm events, so a steady fan goes quiet. Nothing about the firmware's view changes: frames still come only from Plant.Frame/Current.
- **File count (10, at the warning threshold; kept, not split; checker scope warning, iteration 2):** four of the ten files are new tests or a fixture: engine/surfaces_test.go, supervisor/surfaces_test.go, model/derive_test.go and testdata/fans-v2/load-test.toml. engine.go and events.go are edited by both tasks, because tach and load go through the same PortState pointer, emit and Kinds pattern. A split would give two plans that edit engine.go, events.go and control.go in sequence. That adds a wave and no parallelism, and the first plan would leave PortStatus with half its D-13/D-16 fields. Each task stays at 6-7 files, and the estimate is under the smart-zone warning line.
- **Assumption A-EXP (Claude's discretion):** exponents fixed at 1 and 3.
- **Order (renumbered at plan revision, formerly 04-05):** after 04-04 (Plant.Reading, engine.go) and 04-05 (both edit internal/control/control.go, internal/supervisor/supervisor.go and internal/cli/cli.go).
- **Seed:** two engines with the same Config.Seed are deterministic on the Manual clock (late ticks are not possible there; tick-pattern invariance is proven at the plant level in 04-04).
- **Pattern analogs:** 04-PATTERNS.md sections "engine.go" (build, emitRPM/emitSample, Apply replug), "events.go" (Kinds, Sample), "control.go, supervisor.go" (PortStatus, status mapping), "cli.go" (WatchCmd/HistoryCmd help) and "plant.go, tach.go, curve.go, noise.go, derive.go" (derive.go). Per "Plan file-name mapping": derive_test.go → fan_test.go conventions; engine/surfaces_test.go → engine_test.go harness; supervisor/surfaces_test.go → supervisor/watch_test.go (watchSup, startWatch); testdata/fans-v2/load-test.toml → fans/*.toml and RESEARCH Code Example 5.
