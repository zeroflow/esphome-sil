## Planning notes (recorded decisions and assumptions)

- **S7b criteria (RESEARCH "Spike S7 scheduling", adapted to a 25 s live window):**
  - (a) the mean of the readings is within 5 rpm of 1100. 20 readings bound the edge effect of the two half-filled end windows at about 60/N rpm; S7a checks the 1 rpm bias over thousands of readings.
  - (b) the peak-to-peak spread is at most 90 rpm (allows 10 ms of jitter, Assumption A6).
  - (c) at least 2 distinct levels appear: 1100 rpm is 36.67 pulses/s, so an ideal tach would give one level.
  - (d) step signature, toggle half (roadmap criterion 3 as amended at plan revision): peak-to-peak >= 12 rpm, and the readings below their mean and those at or above it each make up at least 25 %. A scratch emulation of the firmware window at plan revision gave a minimum spread of 15 rpm (at the half-step window phase) and a minimum minority share of 33 % over 25-reading windows for jitter 0..10 ms. The lattice half (readings on the 30 rpm grid or one phase-offset copy of it) needs a jitter-free window and is gated hermetically by S7a in 04-04.
  - The on-grid fraction is logged only (RESEARCH Pitfall 1).
- **Fallback rule (CONTEXT carried forward: analytic quantisation is the fallback):**
  - If (b) fails in three consecutive runs while S7a (04-04) passes, the cause is firmware-side processing jitter. A failure of (d) is not a fallback trigger: it means the step signature is not met. Report it with the numbers; do not loosen the thresholds.
  - Make Plant.Frame of a tach fan return the last completed 1 s gate reading (held between gates) instead of the 50 ms pulse drain. Status tach_rpm is unchanged; protocol stays 1; the extra second of lag is accepted.
  - Adapt S7a to the held value, re-run S7a and S7b, and add a row to PROJECT.md Key Decisions with the measured numbers.
  - If S7b passes, internal/model/plant.go, internal/model/s7_test.go and .planning/PROJECT.md stay untouched; they are listed in files_modified only for this path.
- **Test order:** pytest runs the files alphabetically, so test_tach_steps.py runs after test_contract.py and test_identity.py on the shared ct-main instance. It puts port 3 back to the default fan and switches Fan 3 off in a finally block.
- **Contract bench locality:** the session fan dir lives in the bench's private runtime dir (0700, removed at teardown); nothing is written to the repo or the user's state.
- **Real-bench page check (moved from the page plan, formerly 04-06 Task 3, at plan revision):** TestWebLoadReadouts needs Docker, the firmware and 04-08's e2e default pin, so it runs here after both the page (04-07) and the shipped library (04-08). It sets its own SIL_FAN_DIR, which wins over the default pin because env.Extra entries appended later win.
- **README additions from the plan revision:** the amended D-04 evidence rule for `datasheet` (page values, recorded derivations from page figures, official vendor datasheet PDFs wherever hosted); measured.tolerance carries provenance and the other [measured] keys do not; tach.noise_rpm is the standard deviation of a 1 s reading.
- **Pattern analogs:** 04-PATTERNS.md section "Test pinning" (silbench _DROP_ENV; the contract test asserts the step signature instead of exact multiples of 30). Per "Plan file-name mapping": tests/test_tach_steps.py → tests/test_api_smoke.py (api_main usage); testdata/fans-v2/s7-steady.toml → fans/*.toml and RESEARCH Code Example 5; e2e/web_test.go → its own TestWebPlot; plant.go/s7_test.go (fallback path only) → "plant.go, tach.go, curve.go, noise.go, derive.go".
