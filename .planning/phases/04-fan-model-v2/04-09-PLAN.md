## Planning notes (recorded decisions and assumptions)

- **Split from 04-08 at plan revision iteration 2 (checker scope warning: 04-08 was at 0.95 of the smart-zone budget):** these subtests were 04-08 Task 2 step 4, and their content is unchanged except for the start boundary below. 04-08 leaves every suite green and TestLibraryV2 with fans_schema2 and status_v2. This plan only adds subtests to the same function, so it shares the e2e-libv2 instance (one firmware build and one `sil up` for all five subtests).
- **Start boundary at or above (user decision 2026-11-11, D-11 amended, ROADMAP criterion 2 and MODEL-06 reworded):** the iteration-1 subtest started the fan at 12 %. It now starts at exactly 10 %, the shipped Arctic's start.duty, so the e2e check sits on the boundary itself. The firmware's speed fan sets the output to 10/100 in float32, and the sim-bus writer sends a float32 in its shortest form (components/sim_bus/sim_protocol.h `Frame::num`, std::to_chars), so the duty arrives as `0.1` and parses to the same float64 as start.duty 0.10. The subtest asserts that the duty event's v is exactly 0.1. If not, the boundary is not under test and the subtest fails with that message instead of passing on a different duty. 8 % stays the below-start check.
- **Observation through `sil` (CLAUDE.md):** status and watch carry every value asserted here. modelSetFan commands Fan N through the firmware's native API, the "commandable" use CLAUDE.md allows, exactly as e2e/model_test.go does. No subtest reads a firmware sensor through the API.
- **Timing from sim time:** the start-delay window [400 ms, 420 ms] is measured between two watch events' t fields (µs of sim time), so host load does not widen it; the 20 ms margin is two 10 ms engine ticks.
- **Noise is on the tach only (D-03 as amended):** shaft rpm, airflow and power are noise-free, so the curve and fan-law assertions are tight. tach_rpm carries 20 rpm of noise: within 120 rpm of rpm is one 30 rpm step plus 4.5 standard deviations.

Confidence is low (estimate-calibration factor 1, 0 samples).
- **Pattern analogs:** 04-PATTERNS.md "Test pinning" and "Plan file-name mapping" (e2e/library_v2_test.go → e2e/model_test.go and e2e/fans_test.go): modelAPI and modelSetFan, env.WaitStatus, watchStart and until.
