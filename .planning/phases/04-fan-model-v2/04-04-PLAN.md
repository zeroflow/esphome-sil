## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (not in user-story form; surfaced for review, no story invented per the MVP rules): see 04-01-PLAN.md. This plan delivers the simulator side of criterion 3: firmware-visible RPM in pulse steps with seeded noise, deterministic across tick timing, with the seed under the user's control.

<objective>
Give a schema 2 fan a pulse tach. The plant counts pulses from the exact rpm integral with the fan's ppr and drains them into the 50 ms bus frames, so the firmware's 1 s mean shows 30 rpm steps per pulse at ppr 2. Noise comes from a stateless keyed hash of seed, port and sim-time bucket, so traces do not depend on tick timing. The seed is set by `sil up --seed` and shown in status. Spike S7a proves hermetically that the steps survive the firmware's time-weighted window.

Purpose: MODEL-09 and MODEL-10 (D-15; carried-forward decisions: tach as a Go pulse accumulator, protocol stays 1, noise as a keyed hash).
Output: tach and noise in internal/model, engine frames from Plant.Frame, S7a, a lint against the global math/rand source, and seed plumbing from the CLI to status.
</objective>

## Planning notes (recorded decisions and assumptions)

- **Order:** after 04-02 (it owns advance and Integral, which the pulse count integrates) and after 04-03 (both touch internal/cli/cli.go, internal/supervisor/supervisor.go and internal/control/control.go).
- **Frame cadence unchanged (RESEARCH Pitfall 2):** rpmFrameStep stays 50 ms. Smaller bins make the firmware mean worse. Frames are still sent only while connected, at the existing `e.simNow >= e.nextRPM` rule. Frame normalises by the plant time elapsed since the previous Frame, so a late tick or a disconnected stretch still gives the true mean pulse rate.
- **Ideal tach keeps v0.1.0 bit for bit:** a fan with Tach.PPR == 0 takes the one-call Step path of 04-01 (no bucket split), so its shaft trace and bus frames are unchanged.
- **Snap and delay expiry inside a bucket:** advance (04-02) already splits at the start-delay expiry and integrates a coast segment only up to the analytic 0.5 rpm crossing. Splitting at 100 ms bucket edges is the only extra split here (every 1 s gate edge is a bucket edge).
- **S7 schedule (RESEARCH "Spike S7 scheduling"):** S7a here (hermetic, before any live run), S7b in 04-08 (live, through aioesphomeapi on the contract bench). If S7a fails criterion (b), first grid-align the engine's frame cadence (nextRPM on multiples of 50 ms of sim time) and re-run before considering the analytic fallback; record the outcome in the SUMMARY either way.
- **Seed (Assumption A-SEED):** spec.json carries it (written by lifecycle.Up, read by the supervisor); the supervisor copies it into run.json and status `sim.seed`. 0 is a valid seed; nil (no flag) means model.DefaultSeed. The range is capped at 2^53-1 with the registered `invalid_argument` code, so no new apperr code is needed.
- **Wall-clock lint covers tests:** use the Manual clock and `waitUntil` in engine tests.
