## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (not in user-story form; surfaced for review, no story invented per the MVP rules): see 04-01-PLAN.md. This plan delivers the simulator side of criterion 3: firmware-visible RPM in pulse steps with seeded noise of the fan.toml amplitude, deterministic across tick timing, and the hermetic proof (S7a) of the step signature the amended criterion 3 defines. The `--seed` flag that puts the seed under the user's control is 04-05, which runs in parallel.

<objective>
Give a schema 2 fan a pulse tach. The plant counts pulses from the exact rpm integral with the fan's ppr and drains them into the 50 ms bus frames, so the firmware's 1 s mean shows 30 rpm steps per pulse at ppr 2. Noise comes from a stateless keyed hash of seed, port and sim-time bucket, so traces do not depend on tick timing, and its amplitude tach.noise_rpm is the standard deviation of a 1 s reading. Spike S7a proves hermetically that the steps survive the firmware's time-weighted window, including the step signature of roadmap criterion 3.

Purpose: MODEL-09 and MODEL-10 (D-03 as amended, D-13; carried-forward decisions: tach as a Go pulse accumulator, protocol stays 1, noise as a keyed hash).
Output: tach and noise in internal/model, engine frames from Plant.Frame, S7a with the step signature, and a lint against the global math/rand source. The seed plumbing from the CLI to status is 04-05 (split out at plan revision to keep both plans under 15 files).
</objective>

- **Order:** after 04-02, which owns advance and Integral (the pulse count integrates them). This plan no longer touches cli, supervisor or control: the seed task moved to 04-05 at plan revision. 04-05 runs in the same wave (3) in its own worktree, and the two plans share no file. The seed reaches the plants through engine.Config.Seed, declared by 04-01. The tests here pass literal seeds (1, 2, 7).
- **Noise amplitude (D-03 as amended on 2026-11-10, Assumption A4 resolved):** `tach.noise_rpm` is the standard deviation of the noise in one 1 s tach reading, the unit the firmware and Home Assistant show. Each 100 ms bucket therefore carries `noise_rpm·noiseScale·z` with `noiseScale = √(TachGate/NoiseBucket) = √10`, and the ten buckets of a gate average back to noise_rpm. The user asked for about 15-20 rpm against the 30 rpm step, a statement about the 1 s reading, so the amplitude is defined there. The shipped value (20, in 04-08) is two thirds of a step.
- **Step signature (roadmap criterion 3 as amended at plan revision):** the firmware's window phase puts readings on the 30 rpm grid or on one phase-offset copy of it (RESEARCH Pitfall 1), so "every reading is a multiple of 30" is false by design. S7a gates what does hold, modelled in a scratch emulation at plan revision (500/1000/1100 rpm, phases 0..975 ms, jitter 0..10 ms): (d) at 0 ms jitter, at most three residues mod 30, one of them 0 (modelled: at most two); (e) peak-to-peak >= 12 rpm (modelled minimum 15, at the half-step phase) and both sides of the mean >= 25 % (modelled minimum 33 %). 04-09 gates (e) live on the contract bench (S7b).
- **Pattern analogs:** 04-PATTERNS.md sections "plant.go, tach.go, curve.go, noise.go, derive.go", "engine.go", "internal/lint/wallclock_test.go" and "No Analog Found" (noise.go, tach.go). - **Frame cadence unchanged (RESEARCH Pitfall 2):** rpmFrameStep stays 50 ms. Smaller bins make the firmware mean worse. Frames are still sent only while connected, at the existing `e.simNow >= e.nextRPM` rule. Frame normalises by the plant time elapsed since the previous Frame, so a late tick or a disconnected stretch still gives the true mean pulse rate.
- **Ideal tach keeps v0.1.0 bit for bit:** a fan with Tach.PPR == 0 takes the one-call Step path of 04-01 (no bucket split), so its shaft trace and bus frames are unchanged.
- **Snap and delay expiry inside a bucket:** advance (04-02) already splits at the start-delay expiry and integrates a coast segment only up to the analytic 0.5 rpm crossing. Splitting at 100 ms bucket edges is the only extra split here (every 1 s gate edge is a bucket edge).
- **S7 schedule (RESEARCH "Spike S7 scheduling"):** S7a here (hermetic, before any live run), S7b in 04-09 (live, through aioesphomeapi on the contract bench). If S7a fails criterion (b), first grid-align the engine's frame cadence (nextRPM on multiples of 50 ms of sim time) and re-run before considering the analytic fallback; record the outcome in the SUMMARY either way. A failure of (d) or (e) means the step signature of criterion 3 does not hold. Do not loosen those thresholds: record the numbers and report the failure.
- **Board constants (Assumption A11, open):** BoardPPR 2 and the 1 s gate follow the Rev 1.0-3.1 product YAML and sim_sensor.h. The Rev 3.3 YAML has no pulse_counter, so confirmation is a Phase 7/8 hardware item (recorded in ROADMAP).
- **Wall-clock lint covers tests:** use the Manual clock and `waitUntil` in engine tests.
