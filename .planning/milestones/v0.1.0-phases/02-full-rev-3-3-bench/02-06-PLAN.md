## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Goal (not in user-story form; surfaced for morning review, no story invented): the complete Rev 3.3 IO surface can be driven from the CLI. This plan completes success criterion 2 (`sil watch` streams the resulting changes as NDJSON; `sil logs` shows firmware and simulator logs) and the watch half of criterion 3 (LED changes appear in `sil watch`).

<objective>
Give the developer and agents the two live views of a run: `sil watch`, a snapshot-then-delta NDJSON stream of typed change events fed by a supervisor-owned event broker that survives simulator restarts, and `sil logs`, a tail/follow of firmware.log and sil.log through the control API, both cleanly ended by shutdown.

Purpose: CLI-03, CLI-04, D-08, D-12; keeps CLI-06 (both through the one control API on api.sock).
Output: internal/events, engine event emission, supervisor Watch/Logs/FollowLogs and logs.go, streaming routes and Client.Stream, `sil watch` and `sil logs`, e2e TestWatchStream and TestLogs.
</objective>
